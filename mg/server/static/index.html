How this generator works

  1. Climate — large-scale temperature/humidity regions with latitude bands, altitude lapse rate and advected orographic rainfall (rain shadow behind ridges).
  2. Terrain — continent spline → mountain belts → plateaus → islands → volcanoes → hills → detail; then coastal shaping and multi-scale diffusion.
  3. Erosion — thermal hillslope diffusion, stream-power incision (E ∝ AmSn), optional droplet erosion and alluvial deposition.
  4. Hydrology — depression filling, D8 routing with a smoothed flow field, drainage accumulation, watershed labelling, hydraulic-geometry carving (W ∝ Q0.5, D ∝ Q0.4) and lake/ocean surfaces.
  5. Biomes — Whittaker classification with mountain/snow/swamp/mesa specials.
  6. Population — fertility and population fields, stratified vegetation scatter, settlement scoring with Poisson-disk thinning, A* roads and ore veins.
  7. Export — block-level surface profiles, caves, vegetation, structures, Anvil region files, heightmaps and a full map bundle.

Every world is reproducible from its seed + mapgen.json.
